From my bug log4 Oct 2026

Test call · AI receptionist for a made-up plumbing company

A caller mentioned a faint gas smell and got the safety instructions. Then they asked about a leaking sink. The agent logged the callback as not urgent and promised a call back during office hours.

Cause
When the caller changed topic, the agent re-decided urgency from the latest thing they said.
First fix
Told the model to keep the urgent flag. In simulations it still dropped it sometimes.
Fix that held
The callback tool now forces urgent if the call was ever marked urgent. The model can't downgrade it any more.
